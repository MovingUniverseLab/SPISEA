==========================================
Hosek et al. (2020) paper examples
==========================================
These notebooks recreate figures from Section 5 of the SPISEA paper
(`Hosek et al. 2020
<https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020ApJ...905...25H/abstract>`_;
arXiv:`2006.06691 <https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06691>`_).

They use ``get_merged_atmosphere_v1`` on purpose, because that is what
the published figures used. For new work, prefer
``get_merged_atmosphere_v3`` (or ``get_merged_atmosphere_v3bb`` for
compact objects), as in the tutorials.
