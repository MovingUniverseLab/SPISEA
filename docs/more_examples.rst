.. _further_examples:

===================
Further Examples
===================
The notebooks below recreate figures from the SPISEA papers. They are
not part of the beginner sequence. Start with
:doc:`Making a cluster <notebooks/01_make_cluster>` if you are new
to SPISEA.

Hosek et al. (2020)
-------------------
These notebooks reproduce the plots in
`Hosek et al. (2020) <https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2020arXiv200606691H/abstract>`_.

:doc:`HR diagrams and extinction laws <notebooks/papers/hosek2020/fig02_hrd_extinction>`
compares evolution models on an HR diagram and isochrones under different extinction laws.

.. figure:: images/iso_comp_example.png
	    :align: center

:doc:`CMDs at different metallicities <notebooks/papers/hosek2020/fig03_cmds_metallicity>`
shows the same populations in several filter pairs.
The Johnson UBV filters in that notebook are kept so the figure matches the paper.

.. figure:: images/iso_cmd_example.png
	    :align: center

:doc:`Multiplicity and differential extinction <notebooks/papers/hosek2020/fig04_multiplicity_extinction>`
compares clusters with unresolved multiples to clusters with differential extinction.

.. figure:: images/cluster_cmd_example.png
	    :align: center

:doc:`Clusters with different IMFs <notebooks/papers/hosek2020/fig05_imfs>`
compares luminosity functions and black-hole mass functions.

.. figure:: images/imf_comp_example.png
	    :align: center

:doc:`Wolf-Rayet populations <notebooks/papers/hosek2020/fig06_wolf_rayet>`
compares Wolf-Rayet stars from different evolution models.

.. figure:: images/WRstars_example.png
	    :align: center

:doc:`Unresolved cluster spectra <notebooks/papers/hosek2020/fig07_unresolved_spectra>`
builds integrated-light spectra at different ages.

.. figure:: images/clust_unresolved_example.png
	    :align: center
